# 6-Week Community Cleanup Timeline Template

Customize this timeline for your specific event. Fill in your dates and adapt the tasks to your community.

---

## TIMELINE OVERVIEW

```
WEEK 1: PLANNING (Mon-Sun)
├─ Define event scope
├─ Scout location
└─ Finalize budget

WEEK 2: RECRUITMENT LAUNCH (Mon-Sun)
├─ Draft recruitment messages
├─ Create signup form
└─ Send first outreach wave

WEEK 3: RECRUITMENT MOMENTUM (Mon-Sun)
├─ Follow-up outreach
├─ Monitor signups by channel
└─ Second recruitment wave

WEEK 4: CONFIRMATIONS (Mon-Sun)
├─ Stop new signups
├─ Confirm all volunteers
├─ Assign roles
└─ Brief supply coordinator

WEEK 5: FINAL PREP (Mon-Sun)
├─ Scout & safety check
├─ Buy/gather supplies
├─ Final volunteer check-in
└─ Brief event lead + roles

WEEK 6: EVENT + POST-EVENT (Mon-Sun)
├─ [EVENT DAY]
├─ Send thank-you emails
├─ Collect metrics
└─ Plan Cleanup #2
```
